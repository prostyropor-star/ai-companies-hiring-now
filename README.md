## How this list is made

- Source: public job feeds of about 8,000 company career sites (applicant tracking systems), collected 19 November 2026
- AI/ML roles: titles mentioning machine learning, AI, LLM, data science, computer vision, NLP and related terms
- Roles posted in the last 60 days
- Removed: recruiting and staffing agencies, data-labeling gig marketplaces and duplicate postings
